# Eargo 6 vs Eargo 8 — Buyer's Comparison

*Researcher: Pax · Date: 2026-05-15 · For: Thomas*

## Bottom line

The Eargo 8 (launched May 2025) is the current flagship and the only one of the two Eargo still sells new. It adds genuinely useful gen-over-gen upgrades — IP68 (vs IPX7), automatic environmental adaptation ("Smart Sound Adjust"), a Device Scan health check, a 2-week extended-life charging case, and a longer 2-year warranty. The Eargo 6 (January 2022) is discontinued by Eargo and only available used/refurbished through third parties. **Buy the Eargo 8 unless you find a clean refurbished Eargo 6 at a steep discount and your hearing loss is mild — the Eargo 8 is the better device on almost every axis Thomas would care about.**

## What's actually new in Eargo 8

- **Smart Sound Adjust** — fully automatic noise-level adaptation; the Eargo 6's "Sound Adjust" switched profiles by environment, but you still picked noise-reduction strength manually.
- **IP68 rating** — up from IPX7. Dust ingress protection (the "6") is new; water submersion spec is also tighter.
- **Device Scan** — patented onboard health/diagnostics check (no Eargo 6 equivalent).
- **Extended-Life Charging Case** — ~14 full recharges (~2 weeks) vs Eargo 6's ~2 days. Wireless-charging compatible.
- **Improved processing for music & phone-call sound** (per Eargo's launch claims).
- **Broader fitting range** than Eargo 7 (Eargo doesn't publish a direct 6→8 fitting-range comparison, so treat as "at least as wide").
- **Same 2-year warranty** as Eargo 6 (not a regression — but no improvement either).

## When Eargo 6 is the right call

- You find a verified-refurbished pair at a deep discount (well under $1,000) **and** the seller offers a return window.
- Your hearing loss is mild and you mostly need help in quiet environments.
- You don't need a 2-week travel charge case or aggressive IP rating.

## When Eargo 8 is the right call

- You want a device Eargo actually still supports with current firmware, app updates, and replacement parts.
- You travel and need a charging case that goes 2 weeks between top-ups.
- You sweat, work outdoors, or want dust ingress protection (IP68).
- You want hands-off automatic noise handling.

## Caveats / what we don't know

- **Neither model streams Bluetooth audio.** If music/phone-call streaming matters, Eargo's Link earbuds ($799) or a competitor (Jabra Enhance, hear.com Horizon, Phonak Lumity) is the better path.
- **Channel count** isn't publicly disclosed by Eargo for either model in the sources reviewed — call Eargo (855-922-3431) if that spec matters.
- **Insurance coverage** varies by plan; UnitedHealthcare Hearing distributes an "Eargo 8 HC Edition" with lifetime support — worth a call to your carrier.
- **Refurbished Eargo 6 pricing** swings widely on Amazon/eBay and listings are inconsistent on warranty transfer — confirm before buying.
- HearAdvisor's lab tests gave Eargo 6 a **B grade** with weak speech-in-noise and feedback handling. Eargo 8 lab data was not located in this pass; HearAdvisor has a dedicated Eargo 8 page worth checking before purchase.
