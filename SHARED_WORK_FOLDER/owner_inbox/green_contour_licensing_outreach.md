# Green Contour Data — Licensing Outreach Drafts

Two cold-email drafts for the second-attempt outreach. Both:
- State clearly that data is for **internal rendering use only — no redistribution**
- Lead with a concrete use case and volume so the recipient knows there's real money on the table
- End with 4 narrow questions so a reply takes ~2 minutes
- Explicitly invite a "no" so even a decline is useful signal

Pick one of the two opener variants depending on whether you'd rather sound founder-direct (Email A) or business-formal (Email B).

---

## Email 1 — Golf Intelligence (StrackaLine)

**To:** sales@golfintelligence.com / info@strackaline.com (try both — Golf Intelligence is the data-licensing brand, StrackaLine is the parent)
**Subject:** Second try — licensing green contour data for EliteGolfMoments (internal use, no redistribution)

Hi Golf Intelligence team,

I run EliteGolfMoments. We produce physical and digital golf-moment renderings for individual customers, and we're looking to license your green contour / slope dataset to drive the topography in those renders. I submitted the contact form on golfintelligence.com a little while back and haven't heard back — sending this directly in case it lands better.

A few things up front so a reply is easy:

- **Use:** internal only. The contour data feeds our rendering pipeline; we output finished imagery / 3D objects to our customers. **The raw data is never redistributed, resold, or exposed via any API.**
- **Volume:** dozens of courses initially, scaling with order volume. Happy to share a NDA-covered course list.
- **Geography:** US-focused, with occasional international requests.

Four questions to make a reply quick:

1. Do you license for an internal-rendering use case like this, and what are the pricing tiers / minimums?
2. What's your current course count, and is there a way I can check whether specific courses are mapped before signing?
3. What format does the data come in (GeoTIFF / LAS DTM / vectorized contours / rendered slope PNGs / JSON slope grids)?
4. How often are courses re-surveyed?

Even a one-line "we don't license outside of X" reply would help me plan. Thanks for your time.

## Notes / things to verify before sending

- **Company name & use-case description.** Drafts assume EliteGolfMoments and a rendering-pipeline use case based on prior context. Adjust if either is off.
- **Volume figure ("dozens of courses initially").** Swap in a real number if you have one — concrete volumes get replies; vague ones don't.
- **NDA mention.** Signals you're serious without committing you to anything. Remove if you'd rather not.
- **"Second try" framing.** Increases urgency *and* gives the recipient a face-saving reason to reply ("oh sorry, let me find that"). If you'd rather not signal prior contact, drop that line from each subject + first paragraph.
- **Email addresses.** I've guessed the most plausible direct addresses; both companies' actual licensing inboxes may be on their About / Contact pages. Worth checking LinkedIn for a BD or Partnerships contact and emailing them direct — that beats `info@` every time.
