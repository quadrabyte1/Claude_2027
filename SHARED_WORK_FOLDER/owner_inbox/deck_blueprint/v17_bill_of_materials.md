# West Hartford Deck — v17 Bill of Materials

**Date:** 2026-05-09
**Prepared by:** Hollis, Home-Improvement Concierge / Owner's Advocate
**Change from v14:** Framing is fully reoriented. Joists now run **y-direction (perpendicular to house)** spanning the 16'-0" depth in two segments split at a new mid-girder line. Girders are now **two lines parallel to the house (x-direction)** instead of three girders running into the yard — doubled 2×10 PT UC4B (was tripled) because the Pylex 10555 saddle (3.5" wide) cannot seat a tripled 2×10 (4.5" wide) without shimming both sides badly. Pier count increases from 6 to **8** (4 per girder line × 2 lines). A **center joist at x=11'-6" framing** plus a **center divider breaker board** at x=11'-9" finish create a clean mid-deck seam so that every field plank runs parallel to the house in two 11'-9" halves, each butting at the center breaker. Net result: no plank spans more than ~11'-9", the saddle-fit problem is fully resolved, and the center seam is a finished design feature rather than a field cut.

---

## Scope

23'-6" × 16'-3" attached deck on existing concrete patio, West Hartford CT. Maximum 12" above finished grade. Attached to wood-frame house via 2×10 PT ledger on the 23'-6" long (house-parallel) edge. Two doubled 2×10 PT girder lines parallel to house, each on 4 Pylex 50 screw piers — mid-girder at y=8'-0", outer-rim girder at y=16'-0". Face-hung 2×10 PT joists at 12" o.c., perpendicular to house, each spanning in two segments (ledger to mid-girder; mid-girder to outer-rim girder). MoistureShield Vision composite decking parallel to house in two 11'-9" halves with center divider breaker, 4-sided picture frame, composite fascia on 3 exposed sides. Two egress points: left-edge single-step stair (42" wide, v12, unchanged) and far-edge wide step (11'-9" long, v13/v14, unchanged). All hardware ACQ-compatible (hot-dip galvanized or stainless).

### 2b. Girders (Doubled 2×10 PT) — PRIMARY CHANGE

Girders are now **two lines running parallel to the house (x-direction)**, each a doubled 2×10 PT UC4B spanning 23'-0" between pier inboards, made of butted plies over piers with joints staggered between plies.

| Item | Size | Length | Qty | Notes |
|------|------|--------|-----|-------|
| Girder ply — 16-ft piece | 2×10 PT UC4B | 16-ft | **4** | 2 girder lines × 2 plies per line; one 16-ft piece per ply (ply-1 joint over pier at x=15'-4"; ply-2 joint over pier at x=7'-8" — staggered per splice rule below) |
| Girder ply — 8-ft piece | 2×10 PT UC4B | 8-ft | **4** | 2 girder lines × 2 plies per line; one 8-ft piece per ply to complete the 23'-0" run |
| Spare girder ply — 16-ft | 2×10 PT UC4B | 16-ft | **1** | 1 spare board |
| Spare girder ply — 8-ft | 2×10 PT UC4B | 8-ft | **1** | 1 spare board |

> **Order: 5 × 16-ft 2×10 PT UC4B + 5 × 8-ft 2×10 PT UC4B for girders.**
>
> **Splice rule (mandatory):** Butt joints must land **over a pier center**, never midspan. Stagger plies so joints fall on different piers: Ply 1 joints at x=15'-4"; Ply 2 joints at x=7'-8". This stagger ensures the doubled girder is never both plies unsupported at the same cross-section. Do not allow both ply joints to land over the same pier.
>
> **Saddle-fit rationale:** A doubled 2×10 (2 × 1.5" actual = 3.0" total width) fits inside the Pylex 10555 saddle (3.5" interior) with ¼" of clearance per side — shimmed plumb with the ¼" cedar/HDPE shims specified in Section 1. A tripled 2×10 (4.5" wide) cannot seat in this saddle without significant grinding or bearing-face shimming that is neither practical nor approved. This is the primary structural reason v17 uses doubled 2×10 rather than tripled.

## Notes for the Homeowner / Contractor

**A. Order PT lumber early.** Kiln-dried-after-treatment (KDAT) is the right spec — drier, straighter, less post-install shrinkage. May need 1–2 weeks lead time at the yard. Call Richards Building Supply or Curtis Lumber (West Hartford area) early to confirm stock of KDAT SYP in the sizes needed.

**B. v17 structural rationale — why doubled 2×10 and why rotated.** In v14/v15, the girders were tripled 2×10 running into the yard (y-direction). The Pylex 10555 saddle has a 3.5" interior width. A tripled 2×10 (actual 3 × 1.5" = 4.5") is 1" wider than the saddle — it cannot be seated without heavily shimming both sides in a way that's neither stable nor approved by the saddle manufacturer. A doubled 2×10 (actual 3.0") fits within the 3.5" saddle with ¼" per side, properly shimmed with ¼" cedar or HDPE shims per the shim spec in Section 1. Rotating the girders to run parallel to the house (x-direction) allows them to be doubled 2×10 while the joists — now running into the yard (y-direction) at two short 8-ft segments — pick up the load distribution that the wider girder spacing (7'-8" pier spacing vs. the old 11'-6") makes feasible. This is confirmed by DCA-6 Table 3A.

**C. DCA-6 Table 3A girder span check.** The reference table for doubled 2×10 Southern Pine at an 8-ft joist span yields a maximum allowable girder span of 8'-2". v17 pier spacing is 7'-8" — approximately 6" under the limit. Bring the DCA-6 reference to the West Hartford permit office with the plans. The plans examiner will want to see this check, especially because the girder orientation is non-standard.

**D. IRC R507 joist span check.** Per IRC R507 prescriptive spans, a 2×10 SYP at 12" o.c. supports joist spans well beyond 8 ft in the normal residential loading range. The v17 joist segments are each ~7'-11¼" — well within allowable limits. No engineering letter should be required for this span, but confirm with the West Hartford building official.

**E. Saddle shim detail — important for pier installation.** Each Pylex 10555 saddle gets two ¼" shims, one per side, installed before the doubled girder is lowered in. Shims must be: (a) rot-resistant — cedar or HDPE only, not OSB, not plywood, not composite shims of unknown composition; (b) sized to bear the full saddle side-wall face — minimum 1.5" × 3.5" plan area; (c) secured so they cannot walk out during saddle bolt tightening (a dab of construction adhesive on the shim face before placement is acceptable). The ½" HDG lag bolts through girder bottom into saddle (2 per saddle) then lock everything in place. Do not skip the shims — an unshimmed doubled 2×10 in a 3.5" saddle can rack side-to-side under live load.

**F. Joist orientation — brief contractor warning.** In v17, joists run **perpendicular to the house** (into the yard), not parallel. This is the reverse of the typical attached-deck framing instinct where joists run sideways. If a framer defaults to parallel joists here, the whole framing plan is wrong. The girders run parallel to the house (like ledger-parallel beams); the joists bridge between them into the yard. Verify this with the crew foreman before framing begins.

**G. Pier spacing and girder splice — do not deviate.** Pier spacing along each girder line is 7'-8" (four piers creating three bays). Girder plies splice at pier centers only — never midspan. Ply 1 joints at x=15'-4"; Ply 2 joints at x=7'-8". This stagger is mandatory. A contractor who proposes to splice both plies at the same pier location, or to splice midspan to use shorter lumber, should be directed back to the DCA-6 specification.

**H. Center joist and center divider breaker — installation sequence.** Install the center framing joist (at x=11'-6" framing centerline, between the regular joist rows at x=11'-0" and x=12'-0") as part of the regular joist layout. It hangs in LUS210Z hangers at the ledger and both girder faces exactly like all other joists. After decking is installed on both halves up to the center bay, the MoistureShield Vision center divider breaker board is face-screwed down into the top of this center joist with Cortex screws + color-matched plugs at 12" o.c. The breaker provides the finish seam between the two halves of field decking — every field plank butts against the breaker on one end rather than spanning across or having a mid-deck butt joint on a regular joist.

**I. Confirm 12-ft MoistureShield Vision stock at Richards Building Supply (West Hartford) before ordering.** The v17 field plank order of 72 × 12-ft Vision boards is based on 12-ft stock availability. If Richards is out and the next available length is 20-ft, shift to 36 × 20-ft boards (each yields two 11'-9" pieces with 6" waste). Call before the lumber order is placed — do not assume availability on a quantity of 72 boards.

**J. Joist hanger count is substantially higher than v14.** v14 had approximately 75 LUS210Z; v17 requires 100. This is because joists now hang at 4 points each (ledger, both faces of mid-girder, outer-rim girder inboard face) rather than 2 points. Budget and purchase accordingly. Buy in bulk — 100-ct boxes from a fastener supplier are typically available at better unit pricing than 10-ct retail packs.

**K. Joist hanger nail/screw count is correspondingly higher.** 100 LUS210Z × 14 holes each = 1,400 fasteners. Buy 1,500 minimum. The same warning from v14 applies: do not substitute nail sizes or types. The N10 (10d × 1½") or SDS ¼"×1½" spec on the Simpson LUS210Z packaging is the minimum; driving 16d common nails into these hangers does not achieve rated load and will fail inspection.

**L. Joist tape coverage — larger roll requirement in v17.** 50 joist segments at ~8 lf each = ~400 lf joist tape, plus girder tops (~92 lf), plus ledger (~23 lf) = ~515 lf total. Buy 11 × 50-lf rolls of Vycor Deck Protector or equivalent. Apply tape to all framing tops **after framing inspection passes and before the first decking plank is set.** The framing inspection must occur on bare wood — do not tape before the inspector signs off.

**M. Cortex color — order after colorway is selected.** Do not order Cortex plugs before the MoistureShield Vision colorway is finalized. The center divider breaker and all PF boards will have visible Cortex plugs — color match is non-negotiable. Write down the Vision colorway name and Cortex part number once selected and keep it in the homeowner file for future replacement materials.

**N. West Hartford building permit.** This deck is ≥ 200 sq ft attached to the house — a building permit is required. Bring to the permit office: the v17 plan drawings, this BOM, the Pylex 50 + Pylex 10555 saddle load data sheets, the DCA-6 Table 3A girder span check, the Simpson LUS210Z and HU210-2 spec sheets, and the Simpson DTT2Z lateral tie spec sheet. The West Hartford plans examiner will likely ask about the doubled (not tripled) girder — have the DCA-6 table ready.

**O. No railing required by code.** IRC R312 requires guardrail at decks ≥ 30" AG. This deck is ≤ 12" AG throughout. Confirm this interpretation with the West Hartford building department at permit application. A decorative rail is fine if aesthetically desired, but it is not a code requirement.

**P. Seasonal heave on far-edge step — unchanged from v14.** The 5 precast blocks for the far-edge step are not frost-protected. Expect ¼–½" of seasonal lift and resettlement each year. Do not grout blocks in place. Check level each spring.

**Q. End-grain sealing is not optional.** Every cut end of every PT board must be sealed with Copper Green or Anchorseal 2 immediately after cutting. Keep the can at the saw station.
