# West Hartford Deck — Lumber Yard Purchase List

**Preparer:** Hollis | **Date:** 2026-05-11 | **Rev:** v18 / 2026-05-11

**Scope:** Attached 23'-6" × 16'-6" deck, pressure-treated framing on Pylex screw piers, MoistureShield Vision composite decking. This list covers all lumber-yard and specialty-supply items required for framing, decking, connectors, fasteners, and moisture protection. Pylex 50 screw piers are ordered separately — not a lumber-yard item.

## 2. Lumber — Pressure-Treated SYP, Ground-Contact (UC4B)

All PT lumber: Southern Yellow Pine, ground-contact rated (UC4B), KDAT (kiln-dried after treatment) preferred. Verify net dressed length at the yard before delivery.

| Category | Stock Size | Qty to Order |
|----------|------------|--------------|
| Ledger | 2×10 PT 20-ft | 1 |
| Girder plies | 2×10 PT 12-ft | **7** (6 needed + 1 spare) |
| Girder plies | 2×10 PT 8-ft | **5** (4 needed + 1 spare) |
| Joists | 2×10 PT 8-ft | 55 (50 needed + ~10% waste) |
| Blocking (field + house-side) | 2×10 PT cuts from drops | — |

**Girder splice recipe:** Each girder line is a doubled 2-ply 2×10. Ply 1 uses two 11'-6" pieces cut from 12-ft stock (6" waste each); Ply 2 end pieces are 5'-9" cut from 8-ft stock, with an 11'-6" center section also from 12-ft stock. Splices are staggered between plies per standard practice. Order 4 extra 8-ft boards if dedicated blocking stock is preferred over using joist drop-offs.

### 5b. Girder Assembly (Doubled 2-Ply)

Per AWC DCA-6 / NDS, the specified method for laminating a 2-ply 2×10 girder is ½" through-bolts staggered along the full run. **Spec: ½" × 3½" HDG carriage bolt + nut + 2 washers at 24" o.c., staggered top-and-bottom, full girder run both lines.**

Order the step's ribbon lumber and hardware separately when the concrete-block foundation is addressed.
