## Closing Notes

**Far-edge step assembly omitted.** The far-edge step ribbon (doubled 2×6 PT board running along the far rim) was deliberately removed from this list. That step sits on precast concrete blocks, which are out of scope for a lumber-yard purchase order. The step assembly — ribbon, ribbon lag bolts, and ply-to-ply screws — should be addressed in a separate concrete/masonry materials list once the step foundation is finalized.

**Pylex screw piers** are not listed here; they are a specialty pier product ordered separately from a lumber yard. Confirm pier count and order with your pier supplier before framing begins.

**Seal all PT field cuts** with end-grain sealant immediately after cutting — this is the single most skipped step on PT decks and the first place rot starts.
