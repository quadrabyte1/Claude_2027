# West Hartford Deck — Bill of Materials

**Version:** v15 plan, dated 2026-05-06
**Prepared by:** Hollis, Home-Improvement Concierge / Owner's Advocate

---

## Scope

23'-6" × 16'-3" attached deck on existing concrete patio, West Hartford CT. Maximum 12" above finished grade. Attached to wood-frame house via 2×10 PT ledger on the 23'-6" (long) edge. Three perpendicular tripled-2×10 PT girders on 6 Pylex 50 screw piers. Face-hung 2×10 PT joists at 12" o.c., parallel to house. MoistureShield Vision composite decking perpendicular to house, 4-sided picture frame, composite fascia on 3 exposed sides. Two egress points: left-edge single-step stair (42" wide) and far-edge wide step (11'-9" long). All hardware ACQ-compatible (hot-dip galvanized or stainless).

## Notes for the Homeowner

**A. Order PT lumber early.** Kiln-dried-after-treatment (KDAT) is the right spec — drier, straighter, less post-install shrinkage. May need 1–2 weeks lead time. Call Richards Building Supply or Curtis Lumber (West Hartford area) early.

**B. Girder plies are standard 16-ft boards — no yard-net-length question.** The 9 girder plies are exactly 16'-0" — order standard 16-ft 2×10 PT and use at full board length. No special ordering, no trimming.

**C. Side fascia fits 16-ft boards exactly.** Left-side and right-side MoistureShield Vision fascia boards are exactly 16'-0" — order 16-ft fascia boards and use full length.

**D. Side PF boards still need 18-ft stock.** The left-side and right-side PF boards are 16'-3" (they extend from house wall to PF outer edge = 16'-0" rim + 3" cantilever). That's 3" over a standard 16-ft board. Order 18-ft MoistureShield Vision boards for these two pieces, or plan a splice over a joist/blocking point.

**E. Mark outer pier hole positions carefully before punching.** Outer pier row is at y=16'-0". Confirm marks before drilling or punching concrete.

**F. Cortex plug color must match your MoistureShield Vision colorway.** Select your Vision color first, then order Cortex in the matching color. Do not substitute generic composite plugs.

**G. Verify joist-hanger nail spec on Simpson packaging.** LUS210Z calls for N10 (10d × 1½") or SDS ¼"×1½" structural screws. The HU210-3 has its own spec. Read both spec sheets at time of purchase.

**H. Check West Hartford building permit before lumber delivery.** Decks ≥ 200 sq ft attached to house require a permit in West Hartford. Have Pylex load data sheet, lateral tie device (DTT2Z), and joist/girder species/grade ready for plans examiner.

**I. Exposed patio strip — cosmetic note.** The existing patio is 23'-6" × 16'-6". The deck covers 23'-6" × 16'-3" of the patio to the PF outer edge. A 3" × 23'-6" strip of bare concrete patio is visible along the outer far edge.
